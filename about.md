# **Miscellaneous Quotes**

Have you ever felt unmotivated, sad, or needed something to lift your spirits up?

Well do i have the mod for you.

This mod adds:

- **Multiple** Buttons that spits out random quotes each time when clicked
- **Death Quotes** that show a random quote through a notification after a cetain number of deaths

There are over 40+ quotes that can be chosen, some of these are actually motivational quotes that will (hopefully) motivate you, and others are quotes from GD memes and charaters in tv shows.

With these quotes, you will be motivated to do anything you want to do!!!!!

If you want to don't like where a button is, or want to disable death quotes, you can do that in the settings.

This is also my first mod so uhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh *yeah*

Have fun with this mod and stay motivated!!
